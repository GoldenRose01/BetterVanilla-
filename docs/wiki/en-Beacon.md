# Beacon bonus

Languages: [Italiano](Beacon.md) | [English](en-Beacon.md) | [Español](es-Beacon.md) | [简体中文](zh-Beacon.md) | [हिन्दी](hi-Beacon.md) | [العربية](ar-Beacon.md) | [Português](pt-Beacon.md) | [বাংলা](bn-Beacon.md) | [Русский](ru-Beacon.md) | [اردو](ur-Beacon.md)

A maximum-level beacon can grant **Saturation V** while Regeneration is selected.

## Activation

1. Build a complete level 4 beacon pyramid.
2. Select Regeneration as either the primary or secondary effect.
3. Drop a golden apple directly onto the center of the beacon block.

The apple is consumed. Players within **50 blocks** receive Saturation V. The bonus requires maximum level, Regeneration selected and the apple directly above the beacon.

A golden apple dropped elsewhere or on another kind of beacon does not trigger this effect.
