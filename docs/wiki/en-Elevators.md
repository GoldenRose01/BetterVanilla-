# Elevators

Languages: [Italiano](Ascensori.md) | [English](en-Elevators.md) | [Español](es-Elevators.md) | [简体中文](zh-Elevators.md) | [हिन्दी](hi-Elevators.md) | [العربية](ar-Elevators.md) | [Português](pt-Elevators.md) | [বাংলা](bn-Elevators.md) | [Русский](ru-Elevators.md) | [اردو](ur-Elevators.md)

Elevators are separate blocks with dedicated textures in the `elevators` namespace. Resource packs that change vanilla blocks do not automatically change elevators, but may explicitly override their assets.

## Use

- Interact with an elevator to find the nearest elevator above it.
- Hold **Shift** while interacting to search downward.
- Both elevators must share X/Z coordinates, within **128 blocks**.
- The destination needs two clear blocks above it. If obstructed, teleportation does not occur.
- Every successful teleport plays Enderman and Shulker teleport sounds.
- When a player is within **15 blocks**, nearby elevators emit particles matching the block.
- No item frames or marker blocks are needed.

Mobs cannot spawn on elevator blocks themselves.

## Variants

The **Elevators** creative tab, with the Copper Elevator as its icon, contains:

- Wool: white, orange, magenta, light blue, yellow, lime, pink, gray, light gray, cyan, purple, blue, brown, green, red and black.
- Copper: copper block.
- Chiseled blocks: sandstone, stone bricks, quartz, red sandstone, polished blackstone, nether bricks, tuff, tuff bricks, resin bricks, deepslate, sulfur and cinnabar.

All variants connect to one another; destination materials need not match. See recipes for crafting and installation for requirements.
