# Cauldrons

Languages: [Italiano](Calderoni.md) | [English](en-Cauldrons.md) | [Español](es-Cauldrons.md) | [简体中文](zh-Cauldrons.md) | [हिन्दी](hi-Cauldrons.md) | [العربية](ar-Cauldrons.md) | [Português](pt-Cauldrons.md) | [বাংলা](bn-Cauldrons.md) | [Русский](ru-Cauldrons.md) | [اردو](ur-Cauldrons.md)

Drop items into a **water-filled cauldron**. The entire stack converts into the same number of output items.

| Dropped item | Result |
|---|---|
| Dirt | Mud |
| Coarse dirt | Mud |
| Rooted dirt | Mud |
| Concrete powder of any color | Concrete of the same color |

Water is required: empty or lava-filled cauldrons do not activate conversions. The mod processes dropped item entities in the world; no interface or crafting recipe is needed.
