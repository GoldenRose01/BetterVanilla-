## Elevators do not activate

Check that the block is a BetterVanilla+ elevator and the destination shares X/Z coordinates, is in the selected direction and within 128 blocks. It needs two clear blocks above it.

## Particles are missing

Particles are a client effect: install the mod on your client and stand within 15 blocks. The effect does not update every tick.

## `/gameset` is unknown or unauthorized

Check that the server loaded the mod and Fabric API and you have `COMMANDS_GAMEMASTER`. Move close to the villager; the editor command searches only nearby.

## The beacon does not grant Saturation

Check level 4, Regeneration as a primary or secondary effect and a golden apple dropped directly on the beacon. Players must be within 50 blocks.

## The cauldron does not convert items

A water cauldron is required. Supported items are dirt, coarse dirt, rooted dirt and concrete powder.

## The JAR does not start

Minecraft, Fabric Loader, Fabric API and JAR must all match: 26.3 or 26.4 Snapshot 7. See installation and compatibility.
