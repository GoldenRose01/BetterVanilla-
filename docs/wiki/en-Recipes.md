# Recipes

Languages: [Italiano](Ricette.md) | [English](en-Recipes.md) | [Español](es-Recipes.md) | [简体中文](zh-Recipes.md) | [हिन्दी](hi-Recipes.md) | [العربية](ar-Recipes.md) | [Português](pt-Recipes.md) | [বাংলা](bn-Recipes.md) | [Русский](ru-Recipes.md) | [اردو](ur-Recipes.md)

Each elevator has its own shapeless recipe. Combine:

1. One matching vanilla base block.
2. One Ender Pearl.
3. One Redstone.

The result is **one elevator** of that material. White Wool + Ender Pearl + Redstone produces one White Wool Elevator. Ingredient placement in the grid does not matter.

This applies to all sixteen wool colors, the copper block and all twelve chiseled blocks. Ingredients are consumed as in a normal recipe. See elevators for the variant list and teleportation instructions.
